# Product Context — Tocamatrol

## What Tocamatrol Is
Tocamatrol turns an iPhone or iPad into a customizable macro pad and wireless two-finger trackpad over bonded Bluetooth LE to an inexpensive **ESP32-S3 USB dongle**. The dongle enumerates to any Windows, macOS, or Linux computer as a standard USB HID keyboard and mouse—with zero PC drivers, companion apps, or network dependencies.

## Target Audience & Scene
Developers, creators, CAD/radiology/video operators, presenters, and desk power-users using an iPhone propped below a monitor or an iPad beside a keyboard on bright daytime desks and dim editing rooms alike. Buttons must scan instantaneously under peripheral vision, register touch-down physically, and remain clear across dense 8×6 grids.

## Surfaces
- **Operate (iOS / iPadOS app):** Live macro pad (custom grid sizes, multi-cell spans, custom tints/typefaces/captions, last-sent readout), full-screen 2D Magic Trackpad with inertial scroll & multi-finger clicks, Page & Grid Editor, Step-by-Step Macro Editor, Virtual Keyboard Macro Recorder, Dongle Pairing Manager, and Settings (with Paper / Midnight / Auto appearance & Face ID lock).
- **Persuade & Utility (Web Flasher & Docs at `ryancummings.github.io/tocamatrol`):** Open-source overview, ESP32-S3 hardware compatibility guide, browser Web Serial `.bin` firmware flasher, BLE pairing walkthrough, and privacy policy.

## Brand Commitments
- **Visual World:** **Ink & Pop Comic Utility** — Bright Newsprint (Light default) and Dark Electric Comic (Dark) with bold India-ink / electric keylines, crisp hard-offset press-slam shadows, subtle Ben-Day halftone textures, and vivid Silver-Age / CMYK Pop-Art key tints.
- **Voice & Copy:** Direct, plain-English technical utility. Controls name their action; hardware limits and errors name the cause and recovery.
- **Non-negotiables:** Open-source MIT license, zero telemetry, zero driver software, bonded BLE security only, 44×44pt minimum touch targets on iOS, full accessibility label preservation.
