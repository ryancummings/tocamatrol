Tocamatrol is a remote control for a computer. It needs a small USB dongle: an ESP32-S3 board (for example a LILYGO T-Dongle-S3) flashed with our free, open-source firmware from https://ryancummings.github.io/tocamatrol/. The dongle plugs into a Mac, Windows or Linux computer and appears to it as a standard USB keyboard and mouse. The app sends keystrokes, pointer movement and scrolling to the dongle over bonded Bluetooth LE. There is no account, login, server or in-app purchase. Source code: https://github.com/ryancummings/tocamatrol

Without a dongle, the macro pad and trackpad cannot send input, and the status shows "No dongle". Everything else in the app works and can be reviewed:

1. Keys tab: the Starter page shows example macro buttons. Tap an empty cell (+) to put a macro on it.
2. Tap Edit (top right) to open Pages & Macros. Add, rename, duplicate, reorder or delete pages.
3. Open a page to edit its grid: change the grid size up to 8x6, hold and drag a button to move it, and drag the corner grip to resize it. Tap a button to change its color, typeface and caption.
4. In Pages & Macros, tap + (New Macro) to build a multi-step macro from keyboard shortcuts, typed text and timed pauses, or record one with the built-in keyboard.
5. Pad tab: the trackpad surface and gesture guide. Settings (gear): theme, trackpad speed and scrolling options, export or import pages and macros as a JSON file, and an optional Face ID / passcode lock.
6. Tap the dongle chip (top left) to open the Dongle screen, which lists nearby dongles and pairs with one.

Bluetooth permission is used only to find and connect to the dongle. The app collects no data.
